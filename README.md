# RapidCoach
(Production: Early access — invite-only onboarding)

**RapidCoach™** is an all-in-one, mobile-first SaaS app for independent sport and performance coaches — solo private practices, small training groups, and multi-coach academies. It replaces the usual patchwork of calendars, spreadsheets, paper attendance sheets, and parent group texts with a single app where the schedule, roster, attendance, notes, and family communication are all tied to the session itself.

🔗 **Live site:** https://rapidcoach.com

***Features:***
- **Coaching calendar** — drag-and-drop scheduling across day/week/month views, recurring sessions, color-coded categories, session lengths from 15 minutes to a full day, and automatic double-booking prevention
- **Self-booking page** (optional, per coach) — clients book from coach-defined availability, with pay-at-booking or bill-later per session type
- **Athlete profiles** — sport, school, grade, guardian contacts, practice history, notes journal, and benchmarks on one page; CSV roster import; active/inactive status
- **Attendance tracking** — present/late/absent with late minutes, marked from a phone in a few taps, plus attendance-rate reports by athlete over any date range
- **Practice planner** — build a custom drill library and compose reusable practice plans (warm-up / main set / cool-down); plans snapshot into a session so athlete history never changes retroactively
- **Individual training plans** — a personal training calendar per athlete, assignable to one athlete, a group, or the whole roster, with weekly repeat and week-forward copy
- **Video analysis** — record or upload clips, scrub, slow to 0.25×/0.5×, and step frame-by-frame; clips stay local until saved to the athlete's profile
- **Performance benchmarks** — custom test library with per-test units and direction, tracking latest result, personal best, and trend
- **Session & athlete notes** — a dated, editable coaching journal that rolls up onto each athlete's page
- **Family communication** — rich-text email to one family, a group, or the full roster with delivery/open/bounce tracking and built-in unsubscribe; opt-in one-way SMS for cancellations and schedule changes
- **Waivers & e-signatures** — send waivers to an athlete, group, or roster; parents e-sign from any device; live signed-vs-outstanding tracking, reminders, and downloadable signed PDFs with a timestamped signature certificate
- **Document storage** — shared library for PDFs, spreadsheets, Word docs, and images with expiring download links
- **Read-only parent portal** — passwordless one-time-link sign-in, branded with the coach's logo; families see their athlete's schedule, shared training plan, and pending waivers, scoped to their own athlete only
- **Payments** — Stripe Connect onboarding with payouts direct to the coach's bank; session fees, prepaid session packs, season passes, and recorded cash payments
- **Multi-coach teams** — invite coaches by shareable link, share athletes/groups across the staff or keep them private to one coach, with per-coach calendars and permissions
- **Calendar sync** — Google, Apple, and Outlook

Tiered plans (Standard / Pro / Pro Plus) scale coach seats, athlete limits, and email/SMS volume.
